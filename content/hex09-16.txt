@9 | Small Taming | The Taming Power of the Small
key: restraint; small steps; patience; gentleness; accumulation
core: a time when small, gentle influences restrain great energy, and progress comes through patience and detail
now: Dense clouds gather but no rain falls yet. Your energy or plans are being held back by circumstances that are small but persistent. You cannot force a breakthrough now, but gentle, steady influence can shape what happens next. Small Taming asks you to refine details, improve your approach and build up resources. Like wind slowly shaping the sky, gentleness and persistence achieve what force cannot. The rain — the release — will come when enough has accumulated.
dir: The situation may be moving toward a phase of gentle restraint, when small adjustments and patience achieve more than big moves.
do: Focus on details and small improvements; Use gentle persuasion rather than force; Save energy and resources for later
avoid: Frustration at slow progress; Trying to force a big breakthrough now
love: Small kindnesses matter more than grand gestures right now. Be patient with differences.
work: Refine your plans and polish the details. A major launch may need to wait until conditions improve.
self: Build habits in small increments. Gentle consistency will add up.
jung: Small Taming can symbolize the way a small but persistent attitude, such as a gentle feeling or quiet value, can restrain a powerful drive. The psyche is not changed by force but by repeated, patient attention.
ex: Saving a little money every month, gradually persuading a skeptical colleague, or slowly recovering from an injury.
1: Return to your own path. You have not gone wrong; going back to what is right brings good fortune.
2: You are drawn back to the right way along with others. Returning together brings good fortune.
3: The spokes come off the cart; husband and wife roll their eyes at each other. Conflict arises when you push against restraint. Do not force the issue.
4: If you are sincere, worry departs and danger is avoided. Honest intentions protect you.
5: Sincerity binds people together, and shared wealth with neighbours. Loyalty creates mutual benefit.
6: The rain has come and there is rest. Accumulated virtue has borne fruit. But do not push further: the moon is nearly full, and persistence now brings danger.

@10 | Treading | Conduct
key: conduct; courtesy; caution; risk; respect
core: a delicate situation, like stepping on the tail of a tiger, where courtesy and careful conduct keep you safe
now: The image is stepping on the tail of a tiger without being bitten. You may be dealing with a powerful person, a sensitive situation or a risky step. The traditional text says this can succeed — through careful, courteous and sincere conduct. Know your place and the power of those around you, but do not be paralysed by fear. Good manners, clear boundaries and inner calm let you move through danger safely.
dir: The situation may be moving toward a delicate phase. Courtesy, respect for others and careful conduct will be essential.
do: Behave with tact and courtesy; Know clearly what you value and what your boundaries are; Move carefully when others hold power
avoid: Overconfidence in a risky situation; Provoking someone who is more powerful
love: Be respectful and considerate. Small gestures of courtesy prevent unnecessary hurt.
work: Handle powerful people diplomatically. Know the rules and follow them while pursuing your goals.
self: Pay attention to how you conduct yourself, not just what you want. Your manner shapes the outcome.
jung: Treading the tiger's tail may mirror our relationship with powerful instinctive forces. They cannot be denied, but they can be approached with respect and awareness rather than recklessness.
ex: Negotiating with a demanding boss, approaching a difficult conversation with a family member, or entering an unfamiliar culture.
1: Simple conduct. Moving forward plainly and honestly, without ambition, brings no blame.
2: Treading a smooth, level path. The quiet, unassuming person who keeps to the middle way has good fortune.
3: A one-eyed person who thinks they can see, a lame person who thinks they can walk: they step on the tiger's tail and are bitten. Overestimating yourself brings misfortune.
4: Stepping on the tiger's tail with great caution. Careful, attentive conduct brings good fortune in the end.
5: Resolute conduct. Being determined is necessary, but be aware of the danger it brings.
6: Look back at how you have conducted yourself and examine the signs. If your conduct has been complete and sincere, great good fortune follows.

@11 | Peace | Harmony
key: harmony; prosperity; cooperation; balance; flourishing
core: a time of peace and harmony, when communication flows and things can flourish
now: Heaven and earth unite, and all things flourish. Peace describes a period in which forces that are often opposed — high and low, strong and gentle — are in communication. Relationships improve, work moves forward and the small departs while the great arrives. It is a time of good fortune, but the text also hints that peace must be cared for: harmony is a living balance, not a permanent possession. Enjoy it, share it and use it to strengthen foundations.
dir: The situation may be moving toward greater harmony and cooperation, a time when things can flourish if you keep communication open.
do: Strengthen relationships while things are good; Share success and include others; Use calm times to build lasting foundations
avoid: Complacency; Assuming harmony will last without care
love: A good time for closeness, understanding and mutual support. Nurture it with honest communication.
work: Cooperation and teamwork thrive. Expand carefully and build on shared success.
self: Enjoy a sense of balance. Use it to establish habits that will support you in harder times.
jung: Peace may symbolize a state in which conscious and unconscious are in communication rather than conflict. The upper and lower parts of the psyche meet and exchange energy, often experienced as wellbeing and creativity.
ex: A family reconciling after tension, a team that finally works well together, or a period of stability after a difficult year.
1: Pull up one blade of grass and others come with it. Moving forward together with like-minded people brings good fortune.
2: Bear with the uncultured, cross the river resolutely, do not neglect what is distant and do not form cliques. Broad-minded tolerance keeps to the middle way.
3: There is no plain without a slope, no going without a return. Change is inevitable. Stay firm and sincere in hard times, and you will still enjoy your good fortune.
4: Fluttering down, not relying on wealth, joining with neighbours sincerely. People unite because they share real trust.
5: The emperor gives his younger sister in marriage. Humble, generous union brings blessings and great good fortune.
6: The city wall crumbles back into the moat. Peace is ending; do not use force. Accept the change and attend to your own circle.

@12 | Standstill | Stagnation
key: stagnation; obstruction; integrity; withdrawal; patience
core: a period of standstill, when communication breaks down and integrity matters more than advancement
now: Heaven and earth no longer communicate. Standstill describes a time when things do not move: people misunderstand each other, efforts produce little and the wrong influences may be gaining ground. The text advises the superior person to hold back, keep to their values and not be tempted by rewards from unworthy sources. This is not a permanent condition. Standstill always ends, and those who keep their integrity will be ready when things move again.
dir: The situation may be moving toward a period of standstill, when patience and integrity matter more than pushing forward.
do: Keep your integrity and values; Withdraw from unhealthy influences; Prepare quietly for when things change
avoid: Compromising your principles for short-term gain; Forcing communication that is not being received
love: If communication has broken down, do not push. Take time to understand what is blocking connection.
work: Progress may stall. Use this time to reassess, protect your reputation and avoid questionable deals.
self: You may feel stuck. Hold to what you know is right and trust that stagnation passes.
jung: Standstill can describe a psychological state in which the flow between conscious and unconscious is blocked: life feels flat and meaningless. Such periods can be a necessary pause before renewal.
ex: A workplace where management and staff no longer listen to each other, or a relationship stuck in the same argument.
1: Pull up one blade of grass and others come with it. Withdraw together with like-minded people. Staying firm brings good fortune.
2: Patience and compliance help ordinary people, but the great person accepts standstill and does not compromise. This leads to success.
3: Hidden shame. Recognizing what you have done wrong is the first step to change.
4: Acting according to a higher calling, without blame. Those who share the same values will find blessings.
5: Standstill is ending. Remain alert: "What if it should fail? What if it should fail?" Care makes success secure, like tying it to a mulberry tree.
6: Standstill is overturned. First there is obstruction, then there is joy.

@13 | Fellowship | Fellowship with People
key: community; cooperation; shared purpose; openness; friendship
core: a time of fellowship, when open cooperation around shared values achieves what individuals cannot
now: Fellowship describes people united by a shared purpose, like fire rising toward heaven. True community is built on openness and common values, not on private interests or narrow cliques. The text says fellowship "in the open" brings success and makes it possible to cross the great water — to achieve great things together. Look for where you can cooperate, include others and build trust across differences.
dir: The situation may be moving toward cooperation and community. Shared goals and open-mindedness will make progress possible.
do: Look for common ground with others; Be open, inclusive and honest; Work toward shared goals
avoid: Cliques and exclusive loyalties; Hidden agendas
love: Shared values and friendship are the foundation now. Do things together that matter to both of you.
work: Collaborate openly. Build alliances across teams or departments.
self: Connect with communities that share your values. You do not have to do everything alone.
jung: Fellowship can symbolize the integration of different parts of the personality around a common purpose. Outwardly, it reflects the human need for community. Inwardly, it suggests reconciling conflicting aspects of yourself.
ex: Volunteers organizing a neighbourhood event, a team working on a shared project, or friends supporting each other through change.
1: Fellowship at the gate. Open cooperation from the start brings no blame.
2: Fellowship limited to your own clan. Narrow loyalties lead to regret.
3: Weapons hidden in the bushes; watching from a high hill. Suspicion prevents fellowship for a long time.
4: Climbing the wall but not attacking. Realizing you cannot win by force, you turn back — good fortune.
5: Friends first weep and then laugh. After struggle, great forces come together and meet.
6: Fellowship in the meadow outside the city. Not the deepest union, but no regret.

@14 | Possession in Great Measure | Great Possession
key: abundance; success; responsibility; generosity; clarity
core: a time of abundance and success, which lasts when it is handled with modesty, clarity and generosity
now: Fire high in the heavens shines on everything below. This hexagram describes great possession: success, resources or influence. The text promises supreme success. The real question is how you use what you have. Abundance brings responsibility. Modesty, generosity and good judgment keep success healthy, while pride and wastefulness undermine it. Make sure your success serves something good.
dir: The situation may be moving toward success and abundance. It will be important to use your resources wisely and generously.
do: Use resources wisely and generously; Stay modest and grounded; Support good causes and good people
avoid: Arrogance and showing off; Wasting resources
love: Share your good fortune and appreciation generously. Do not let success create distance.
work: A prosperous time. Invest wisely, reward your team and avoid overconfidence.
self: Recognize your blessings and consider how to share them.
jung: Great Possession may point to psychic wealth: talents, energy and insight. The task is to use them consciously, without becoming inflated by them.
ex: Receiving a promotion, an inheritance or recognition, and deciding how to use it responsibly.
1: No relationship with harm yet. Abundance has not caused problems; stay aware of difficulty and remain blameless.
2: A big wagon for loading. You have the means to carry a great deal. Move forward; there is no blame.
3: A prince offers his wealth to the Son of Heaven. Use your resources for a higher purpose; a petty person cannot do this.
4: He does not show off his wealth. Restraint and modesty keep you free from blame.
5: Sincerity that is accessible yet dignified. Natural authority brings good fortune.
6: Blessed by heaven. Good fortune; nothing that does not benefit.

@15 | Modesty | Humility
key: humility; balance; moderation; quiet strength; fairness
core: a time when modesty and quiet strength bring success, balancing too much and too little
now: A mountain hidden within the earth: great strength that does not display itself. Modesty describes a person who has real worth but does not boast. The text says modesty brings success and allows the superior person to carry things through to completion. Modesty here is not low self-esteem; it is a balanced attitude that reduces excess and increases what is lacking. It creates trust and lasting influence.
dir: The situation may be moving toward a phase where modesty and quiet competence achieve more than self-promotion.
do: Let your work speak for itself; Balance excess and deficiency fairly; Listen and learn from others
avoid: Boasting or self-promotion; False humility used to seek praise
love: Show appreciation and avoid trying to look superior. Humility creates closeness.
work: Quiet competence will be noticed. Credit others generously.
self: Measure yourself honestly — not too high, not too low.
jung: Modesty may describe an ego that does not inflate itself, either upward into grandiosity or downward into worthlessness. It is in right relationship to the larger Self.
ex: An expert who listens carefully to beginners, or a team leader who shares credit.
1: Very modest. The superior person crosses great water. Humility makes big undertakings possible.
2: Modesty that comes to expression. Recognized humility brings good fortune.
3: Hard-working modesty. Labour without boasting brings success to completion.
4: Nothing that does not benefit modesty in action. Use humility actively, not as passivity.
5: Without relying on wealth, you win the support of your neighbours. Firm, even forceful, action is justified now when something must be corrected.
6: Modesty that has been recognized. Using it, you can set things right in your own sphere.

@16 | Enthusiasm | Delight
key: enthusiasm; motivation; inspiration; readiness; harmony
core: a time of enthusiasm and inspired energy, which moves people when it is grounded and sincere
now: Thunder bursts from the earth: energy is released and people are stirred. Enthusiasm describes a time of inspiration, motivation and shared feeling. The text says it is good for setting up helpers and putting armies in motion — mobilizing people. Enthusiasm is powerful but needs grounding. Joy that comes from genuine harmony moves people; excitement that is self-indulgent or empty fades quickly. Be sincere, prepared and attentive to the mood of others.
dir: The situation may be moving toward a phase of enthusiasm and mobilization. Inspired energy can move people if it is sincere and well-directed.
do: Share your enthusiasm with others; Prepare and organize so energy is not wasted; Stay connected to genuine purpose
avoid: Complacency and self-indulgence; Overconfidence when things feel easy
love: Bring joy and playfulness into the relationship, but stay attentive to how the other person really feels.
work: A good time to inspire a team or launch a campaign. Plan carefully so energy translates into results.
self: Follow what genuinely excites you, but do not lose yourself in pleasure.
jung: Enthusiasm can describe a surge of psychic energy, often from the unconscious. Such energy can heal and inspire, but can also overwhelm judgement if it is not integrated.
ex: Starting a new hobby with excitement, motivating a team before a big event, or the joy of a celebration.
1: Boasting about one's enthusiasm. Self-indulgent display brings misfortune.
2: Firm as a rock. Recognizing the signs early and acting correctly brings good fortune.
3: Looking upward for enthusiasm, waiting for approval. Hesitation brings regret.
4: The source of enthusiasm. Great achievements are possible; do not doubt and others will gather around you.
5: Chronically ill but not dying. Persistent pressure keeps you from false enthusiasm and helps you survive.
6: Deluded enthusiasm. If you change course even late, there is no blame.
